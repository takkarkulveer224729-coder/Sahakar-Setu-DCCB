# Coop Connect — User Manual

Coop Connect is your co-op's own chat app. You use it in a web browser on a phone,
laptop or PC — nothing to install. This manual explains everything in simple steps.

---

## 1. Getting in

### Open the app
Open the link your co-op gave you (for example `https://complianceofficer.taile031e5.ts.net`)
in a web browser. **Google Chrome** is recommended.

### Create your account (first time)
1. Tap **Create account**.
2. Enter your **full name**, a **username** (3–20 letters/numbers, e.g. `asha.patil`),
   and a **password** (at least 6 characters). Type the password twice.
3. Tap **Create account**. You're in.

### Log in (after that)
Tap **Log in**, enter your **username** and **password**, tap **Log in**.
You stay signed in for 30 days on that device.

### Put it on your phone's home screen (optional)
In Chrome, open the menu **⋮** → **Add to Home screen**. Coop Connect then opens like a
normal app, with the logo as its icon.

---

## 2. Chatting

### Start a chat with one person
1. Tap the **💬 new chat** button at the top.
2. Pick a person from the list. Type your message at the bottom and tap **send ➤**.

### Make a group
1. Tap the **👥 new group** button at the top.
2. Give the group a name, tick the members, tap **Create group**.

### Find a chat
Use the **search box** at the top of the chat list. The **All / Unread / Groups**
buttons filter the list.

### Message ticks
- ✓ sent &nbsp;·&nbsp; ✓✓ delivered &nbsp;·&nbsp; **blue ✓✓** read.

### Reply, copy, delete
- **Reply:** double-tap a message (or use its **▼** menu) → type your reply.
- **Copy / Download / Delete:** tap the **▼** on a message.
- **Delete for me:** removes it from your screen only.
- **Delete for everyone:** (your own messages) removes it for everybody.

### Clear or leave a chat
Tap the **⋮** menu at the top of a chat:
- **Clear chat** — empties it for you only.
- **Delete chat** — removes it from your list (it returns if a new message comes).
- **Exit group** — leaves a group.

## 3. Sharing files and photos

Tap the **📎** button in a chat → **Photos & files**. You can also **drag & drop** a file
onto the chat, or **paste** (Ctrl+V) a copied image. Up to 100 MB per file.
Only people in that chat can open the file.

### 📸 WebSnap — take a live photo
Tap the **📷 camera** button to take a photo with your camera right now and send it.
There's a 3-second timer, camera switch, retake and a caption. It's marked **📸 WebSnap**
so everyone knows it was taken live.

### 📍 Location
Tap **📎** → **Location**:
- **Send current location only** — a one-time map pin.
- **Share live location** — your position updates on the map for **15 minutes, 1 hour or
  8 hours**. Tap **Stop sharing** any time. Keep the app open while sharing.

Every location has an **Open in Google Maps** link for directions.

---

## 4. Voice and video calls

In a one-to-one chat, tap **📞** (voice) or **🎥** (video) at the top. The other person
sees an incoming call and taps **green to accept** or **red to decline**.

During a call you can:
- **Mute** your microphone 🎤
- Turn your **camera** on/off 🎥
- **🖥 Share your screen** (from a PC) — show a document or report; the other person watches
- **Hang up** (red button)

Every call is noted in the chat (missed, declined, or its length). Tap it to call back.

> Calls, screen sharing, WebSnap and location need the secure `https://` link and your
> permission to use the camera/microphone/location the first time.

## 5. Your profile

Tap your **picture** at the top-right of the chat list.

### About (your mood)
Pick a ready-made status (🟢 Available, 🤝 In a meeting, 🚜 On field visit…) or write your
own with emojis. Others see it when they tap your name in a chat.

### Report status
Choose up to **3** report items and tick exactly **which people** may see them. Only those
people see them in your Contact info; everyone else sees nothing.

### Change password
Set a new account password. This signs you out on your other devices.

---

## 6. App lock (privacy screen)

Hide the whole app behind a plain **Secure Login** password screen, so a glance at your
phone shows only a password box — not your chats.

### Turn it on
Profile → **App lock** → type a lock password twice → **Turn on app lock**.

### Using it
- A **padlock** button appears at the top. Tap it to lock instantly.
- It also locks by itself when you reopen the app or come back after a minute away.
- To get back in, type your lock password and tap **Sign in**.

### Turn it off
Profile → **App lock** → **Turn off** (it asks for your lock password).

> **Important:** the lock password is kept only on that device and is separate from your
> account password. If you forget it, the only way back is to clear this website's data in
> your browser (which signs that device out). Your chats stay safe on the server.

## 7. Handy to know

- **Dark mode** turns on automatically with your phone/PC setting.
- **Typing…**, **online** and **last seen** show at the top of a one-to-one chat.
- **Notifications:** allow them when asked, to hear and see new messages.
- **Log out:** the ⬅ exit button at the top of the chat list.

## 8. Common questions

**It won't open / "This site can't be reached".**
The server PC may be off or the link is still starting up. Try again in a few minutes,
with Wi-Fi off, in Chrome. If it continues, tell your co-op's admin.

**I forgot my account password.**
There's no self-reset yet. Ask your admin, or create a new account.

**A photo won't show for someone.**
Only people in that chat can open a shared file. Make sure they're in the chat.

**The camera/microphone won't work.**
Tap the 🔒 icon in the browser's address bar and allow Camera/Microphone, then try again.
These need the `https://` link, not a plain `http://192.168…` address.
